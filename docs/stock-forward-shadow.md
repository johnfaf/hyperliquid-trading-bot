# Stock forward shadow protocol

The successful isolated Alpaca paper drill verifies order handling, not an investment edge. This sidecar uses the shadow worker's existing market access for four allowlisted GET endpoints (calendar, clock, SPY daily bars and SPY quotes). It does not read positions, order history, or the drill account, and does not submit, cancel or manage orders. It cannot run in an order-enabled stock configuration. Neither the existing stock worker nor the BTC, exit, or absorption studies use its decisions.

## Fixed before observation

- First eligible date is the next New York calendar day after registration. The 60-calendar-day window has an exclusive end. The broker calendar, the input code fingerprint, and the dates are frozen. New deployments do not change the study's pin.
- SPY long only. Entry at a crossover from at/below to above the 20-session mean; exit on a completed close below the mean. Compute solely from the previous 21 completed exchange-session closes, with no fill on the signal observation. Use the Alpaca IEX feed and split-adjusted daily bars.
- Capture the decision at regular-session open +15 minutes, and the simulated execution at open +20 minutes. The service starts 15 seconds into each slot, has at most 75 seconds from slot start, and rejects an unverified broker clock, stale/early quote, or spread above 0.5%. The execution quote must come after the decision was recorded. Missing or incomplete observations are not replayed or repaired. Both timestamped quotes and the exact input closes used at the decision are retained privately. An unpaired quote can mark inventory, but cannot trigger a strategy fill.
- Unlevered $1,000 reference capital, maximum $75 committed including modeled fees per entry. Apply entry to the ask and exit to the bid, plus 5 bps adverse slippage and 2 bps fee per leg (stress: 12 and 6 bps). Compare with $1,000 unchanged cash and a $75 passive sleeve entered on the execution quote of the first scorable session under the same costs. Mark all open quantities at hypothetical liquidation value; no terminal close is manufactured. The IEX quote is not a promise of a broker fill. No dividends, interest, queue, depth, impact, or intraday drawdown between observations are modeled.
- An initial screening verdict needs a completed window, at least 90% paired sessions including the final session, known monthly operating costs, and at least ten natural closes under stress. The stressed book, net of prorated operating costs, must beat both benchmarks even after removing its largest closed winner. Otherwise the study is either insufficient or fails this screen. A pass is exploratory evidence, not an estimate of future profit or authorization to trade.

## Operation

The isolated systemd timer polls on US weekday trading hours. It does not backfill after downtime. State and fixed inputs live under `/var/lib/hyperliquid-stock-forward/`; the root-owned dashboard publisher relays only the validated summary to **Research → Stock forward shadow**. Reproduce modeled fills using the pinned code and private `market.sqlite3`; `trace.json` holds deterministic simulated trades, not broker evidence. Preserve the study files and the pinned release together. A report with zero scorable sessions is not proof of zero return.
